Summary of Incompatible Constraints

  • Temporal Causality vs Random K-Fold: Standard random shuffle cross-validation cannot be applied to atmospheric time series because shuffling mixes future data into past training windows, violating causality. Use TimeSeriesSplit or blocked chronological folds instead.
  • IEEE Float32 Parquet vs Text Sentinels: Atmospheric reanalysis utilizes standardized numeric IEEE floats and NaNs. Injecting artificial string sentinels (`?`) corrupts PyArrow binary Parquet schemas.
  • Domain Specific Attributes: Homework datasets from banking (fraud tokens), retail (customer demographics), or medical labs (insulin levels) cannot be fabricated into a physical climate intelligence system, though their underlying ML algorithms are fully compatible.
